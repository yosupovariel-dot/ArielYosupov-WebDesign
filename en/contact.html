FAQ

Things worth knowing before you start

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the scope: a landing page is ready within 2 days, a full business website within a week, and an online store within 2 weeks. These are my standard timelines — and at the start of every project I confirm an exact schedule with you and stick to it.

Will I get full ownership of the website when the project is done?

Yes. When the project is complete, the website is entirely yours — including the files, content and admin access. There's no lock-in to a specific provider and no dependency on me for ongoing operation.

What about hosting and a domain?

I can help purchase and set up a domain and hosting, or work with a provider you already have. All the technical settings are explained clearly so you know exactly what you have and how to maintain it going forward.

Is there support after the site goes live?

Yes. I stay available after launch for fixes, small updates and ongoing questions. You always know who to reach out to — no support tickets, no waiting in line.

What does the payment process look like?

Typically a deposit at the start of the work, with the remaining payment due at project completion and before the site goes live. All terms are agreed in advance, fully transparently, with no surprises along the way.
