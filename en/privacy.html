Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. General

This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected on Ariel Yosupov's website, how it is used, and how to get in touch about it. Browsing the site and contacting the Service Provider constitute agreement to this policy.

2. What Data Is Collected

The site itself does not collect or store any personal data. The "Chat on WhatsApp" button and the email/phone links simply open WhatsApp or your email/phone app with a pre-filled message — any information you then choose to share is sent directly to the Service Provider via WhatsApp, email, or phone, outside of this website. The site does not collect payment details or any sensitive information, and does not require registration or a user account.

3. How the Data Is Used

Any information you share via WhatsApp, email, or phone is used solely to get back in touch with you about your project request. The information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

Inquiry details are kept only for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and project, after which they are deleted or retained only for reasonable business record-keeping purposes.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may make external network requests when the page loads. The site's accessibility menu stores your chosen preferences (such as text size and contrast) in your browser's local storage (localStorage) only, so they're remembered on your next visit — this information is never sent to any server.

6. Your Rights

You may contact the Service Provider at any time to review, correct, or request deletion of any personal information you've submitted, using the contact details below.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect submitted information, though no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The version in effect is the one published on the site.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact yosupovariel@gmail.com or call +972 55-665-8131.
